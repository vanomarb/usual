Before you visit

Which branch is open late?

IT Park is open all night for the night shift. Banilad and Capitol Site open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, every day.

Are the busy times live?

No. They show how busy each branch usually is at this hour on a typical day. Use them to plan your break, not as a live count.
