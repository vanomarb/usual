A coffee bar for your break

Usual started with one idea: a break is short, so your drink should be ready when it starts. You order ahead, pick a branch and a time, and your cup is waiting at the counter with your name on it.

Our three branches sit where Cebu works and studies. IT Park stays open all night for the night shift. Banilad and Capitol Site open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, every day.

The same cup at every counter

We pour one house espresso at all three branches, a medium roast that sits well with milk. Baristas weigh every shot, so your usual tastes the same in Banilad at 7 AM and in IT Park at 3 AM.

Milk
Steamed for each cup, never reheated. Oat and soy are always in the fridge.
Tablea
Cacao tablets from Cebu, melted into milk when you order.
Bites
Ensaymada, banana bread and cheese pandesal, baked every morning.

How we work

  • Made for the time you pick

    We start an order a few minutes before its pickup time, so it reaches the counter fresh, never sitting under a heat lamp or melting over ice.

  • Names, not numbers

    Every cup carries the name you give us. Regulars hear theirs before they reach the bar.

  • A card that pays you back

    One stamp for every drink you order ahead, at any branch. The tenth drink is free, any size, any milk.
