Accessibility

Last reviewed 18 November 2026

Ordering a coffee should be easy for everyone. We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and we test it with a keyboard, a screen reader and at 200% text size.

What we have done

  • Every step of ordering, checkout, sign-in and the forms works with a keyboard. In the order builder, Enter or Space confirms a choice and moves you to the next step.
  • A “Skip to content” link is the first thing on every page, and focus is always shown with a clear outline.
  • Text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and controls and their borders at least 3:1.
  • Order status, bag changes and form errors are announced to screen readers. Errors are written out next to the field, not shown by colour alone.
  • Buttons and choices are at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • If you ask your device to reduce motion, the site turns off its animations.
  • Pages reflow to a single column on narrow screens and when text is enlarged, without scrolling sideways.

What we know isn’t perfect

  • The picture of the app on the home page is described as one image rather than as two working screens.
  • Pickup times refresh while the page is open. If a time you picked runs out, the page says so and asks you to choose another.

Tell us what isn’t working

If anything on the site is hard to use, write to us through the contact form and choose “Accessibility” as the topic. We aim to reply within five working days.

This is a demonstration site, so on it the form sends nothing; the commitments above describe how the running business would respond.
