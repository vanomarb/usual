Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Usual respects your privacy. We handle personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

On this demonstration site, nothing you type is sent to us or to anyone else, because there is no server that receives it. What the site remembers stays in your browser’s local storage, listed in section 3, and you can clear it at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Your account: your first name and the email address or mobile number you sign in with.
  • Your orders: what you order, the branch, the pickup time, the name for the cup and how you chose to pay (GCash, Maya or card). Our payment providers handle the payment itself; we never receive full card or account numbers.
  • Your rewards: your stamps and free drinks.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form, with your name and how to reach you.

2. Why we use it

  • To take, prepare and hand over your orders, and to refund them when needed.
  • To keep your stamp card and saved usual.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To keep the records that tax and other laws require.

We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

3. What this site keeps in your browser

The site uses your browser’s local storage, not cookies, for the features you use. Nothing in it is sent anywhere.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our payment providers, only to process your payment.
  • Our hosting and email providers, who process data for us under written agreements and our instructions.
  • Government authorities, when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is open and delete it within 30 days after you ask us to close it. We keep order and payment records for as long as tax law requires, then delete them. Messages are deleted one year after we answer them.

6. How we protect it

We use organisational, physical and technical measures to protect personal data, limit access to the staff who need it, and train our team on data privacy.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is processed;
  • access your personal data;
  • object to processing;
  • have your personal data erased or blocked;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • receive your personal data in a portable form;
  • be paid damages for harm caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false or unlawfully obtained data; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

8. Our Data Protection Officer

To use any of these rights or ask about this policy, write to our Data Protection Officer through the contact form and choose “Privacy and my data” as the topic. We reply within 15 working days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. See also our Cookie Policy and Terms of Service.
