Cookie Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Usual doesn’t use cookies.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this site, and no scripts from other companies. Fonts and icons are served from this site, so loading a page tells no one else that you visited.

What we use instead

A few features need to remember things between pages, such as your bag and your stamp card. For those, the site uses your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

NameWhat it is forKept until
usual:accountKeeps you signed in: your first name and the email or mobile number you usedYou sign out
usual:bagThe items in your bag, so they are still there on the next pageYou place the order or empty the bag
usual:usualThe drink you saved as your usualYou clear site data
usual:cardYour stamps and free drinksYou clear site data
usual:probeA test entry, written and removed at once, to check that your browser keeps site dataRemoved straight away

These entries are strictly necessary for features you ask for, so we don’t show a consent banner. If your browser blocks site data, the site still works; it just forgets your bag, card and sign-in when you leave.

How to clear it

  • Sign out on your account page to remove usual:account.
  • Place your order or remove each item to empty usual:bag.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings to remove everything at once.

About this demonstration site

Usual is a concept brand, and this is a demonstration site. Everything described here is how the site really behaves: it sets no cookies and sends nothing you enter anywhere.

For how the running business would handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
