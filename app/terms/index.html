Last updated 18 November 2026

Usual is a concept brand. This is a demonstration site: nothing you enter here is sent, and no payments are taken.

The terms below are written as they would apply to the running business, so you can see how ordering ahead would work. On this demonstration site, orders, sign-ins and messages stay in your browser, and no one prepares, charges or answers them.

1. Who we are

Usual is a coffee bar with branches in IT Park, Banilad and Capitol Site, Cebu City, Philippines. “Usual”, “we” and “us” mean the business. “You” means anyone who uses this website, the Usual app or a Usual branch.

2. Ordering ahead

  • An order asks us to prepare drinks and bites for pickup at the branch and time you choose.
  • Pickup times start 10 minutes from the time you order and end 15 minutes before a branch closes. IT Park takes orders at any hour.
  • We keep a ready order at the counter for 30 minutes after its pickup time. After that, ask the barista and we will make it again if we can.
  • If we can’t make something you ordered, for example because an item has run out, we tell you before you pay or refund that item in full.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in Philippine pesos and include VAT. The price you see when you place an order is the price you pay.
  • You can pay with GCash, Maya or a debit or credit card. Payments are handled by our payment providers; we never see or store your full card or account number.
  • This demonstration site never asks for card or account numbers and takes no payment.

4. Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel an order in the app until we start making it. You get a full refund to the way you paid.
  • Once we have started, we can’t cancel the order, but tell the barista if anything is wrong with your drink and we will make it again.
  • Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Act of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 7394).

5. Rewards

  • Each drink you order ahead earns one stamp. Nine stamps earn a free drink of any size, with any milk and extras.
  • Stamps and free drinks have no cash value and can’t be sold, transferred or exchanged for money.
  • If we change the rewards programme, we will post the change on this page at least 30 days before it applies. Stamps you have already earned stay on your card.

6. Your account

You sign in with your email address or mobile number and a one-time code; there is no password. Keep access to that email or number, because anyone who can read the code can sign in. You can sign out at any time. We may close an account that is used for fraud or to abuse the rewards programme.

7. Allergens and dietary needs

Our drinks and bites are made at counters that handle milk, soy, nuts, eggs and gluten. Ask the barista before you order if you have an allergy; we can’t promise that anything is free of an allergen.
